The ideas we need today

  • Center, height, top, and bottom: The center is halfway between an object’s top and bottom.
  • Footprint edges use half the width: A footprint is the top-down area an object occupies.
  • Our coordinate convention: Coordinates are ordered x,y,z.

The rules we will use and check

  • A precise prompt supplies executable detail: A prompt should say what to keep, which named objects to create or update, dimensions, center positions, and what to report.
  • Separate requirement, prediction, and observation: A requirement is what we want.
  • A separate roof needs its own center: When a roof of height r sits on a body top t, roof center=t+r/2.

1. An upright box is 10 high and must rest on ground 0. Find center, bottom, and top.

Show the worked answer after trying

Half-height=5, center=5, bottom=0, top=10. The reason is center ± half-height, not “z always equals height.”

2. A roof is 2 high and sits on body top 10. What is its center? What would go wrong with center 10?

Show the worked answer after trying

Center=10+1=11. Center 10 would put its bottom at 9, overlapping the body by 1.
